Privacy Policy

at Lakeside hospital, Vijayapura, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal details such as your name, phone number, email address, and medical information when you fill out forms, book appointments, or contact us.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide medical services, schedule appointments, improve patient care, and communicate important updates related to your health and treatment.

3. Data Protection

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except when required for medical treatment, legal compliance, or healthcare services.

5. Cookies and Website Usage

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience and analyze website traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data. For any requests, please contact us directly.
